HR Services That Deliver Results

Outcome-focused HR support designed for SMEs. We provide clarity, ensure compliance, and deliver practical solutions that work for your business.

Outsourced HR Support

We act as your dedicated HR function, providing flexible access to experienced consultants who understand your business. This approach reduces risk, improves consistency, and gives you professional HR support without the cost of a full-time hire.

What's included:

  • Unlimited telephone and email advice on day-to-day HR matters
  • Strategic HR planning and people management guidance
  • Regular employment law updates and compliance reviews
  • Support with employee relations issues and case management
  • Access to template documents and policy updates

Employee Relations & Case Management

Managing sensitive employee relations matters requires expertise, objectivity, and a calm approach. We support you through disciplinaries, grievances, investigations, and absence issues, ensuring fair process and legal compliance at every stage.

Our support includes:

  • Disciplinary procedures, from investigation through to outcome
  • Grievance handling with objective guidance and documentation
  • Workplace investigations conducted impartially and thoroughly
  • Absence management, including long-term sickness and capability
  • Attending meetings, chairing hearings, or acting as note-taker

Policies, Contracts & Compliance

Compliant, tailored documentation is the foundation of effective people management. We create policies, handbooks, and contracts that reflect your business needs while ensuring legal compliance and best practice.

We deliver:

  • Bespoke employment contracts tailored to your roles and structure
  • Employee handbooks that reflect your culture and commercial needs
  • HR policies covering all key areas from absence to whistleblowing
  • Policy reviews and updates to keep you compliant with changing legislation
  • Settlement agreements and compromise documentation

Performance & Absence Management

Addressing performance issues and managing absence effectively requires clear frameworks, good documentation, and confident manager support. We help you handle these challenges fairly and constructively.

How we help:

  • Performance improvement plans and capability procedures
  • Absence management frameworks and return-to-work processes
  • Coaching managers to have difficult conversations effectively
  • Documentation support to ensure consistent record-keeping
  • Guidance on reasonable adjustments and occupational health referrals

Redundancy & Restructuring Support

Redundancy and restructuring are among the most challenging HR situations. We provide comprehensive support through planning, consultation, and implementation, ensuring legal compliance while handling these sensitive processes with professionalism and care.

Our support includes:

  • Strategic planning and business case development
  • Consultation process design and implementation
  • Selection criteria and scoring methodology
  • Redundancy calculations and settlement agreements
  • Full documentation and compliance throughout the process

Recruitment Support

Hiring the right people is critical to business success. We provide practical recruitment support to help you attract, assess, and onboard talent effectively, without the need for expensive recruitment agencies.

We can help with:

  • Job descriptions and person specifications that attract the right candidates
  • Advert copywriting and posting guidance
  • CV screening guidance and shortlisting support
  • Interview structure, competency frameworks, and question design
  • Offer letters, onboarding plans, and new starter documentation
