Meet the Team

Experienced HR professionals dedicated to supporting SMEs with practical, compliant advice and trusted guidance.

Alex Harwood

Founder and Principal HR Consultant

Alex is an experienced HR partner specializing in supporting SMEs and owner-managed businesses. With a background spanning employee relations, compliance, and strategic people management, Alex founded the consultancy to provide accessible, pragmatic HR support to growing businesses.

Known for clear communication and a calm approach to sensitive matters, Alex has built a trusted, referral-led consultancy based on strong client relationships and consistently delivering practical outcomes.

Areas of expertise include employee relations case management, restructuring and redundancy support, employment law compliance, and advising business leaders on building effective people practices.

Our Associates

Sarah Mitchell

Employee Relations Specialist

Sarah brings over 15 years of experience handling complex employee relations matters. She specializes in disciplinaries, grievances, workplace investigations, and absence management, with a reputation for objective, empathetic case handling that achieves fair outcomes.

James Patterson

Compliance and Policy Specialist

James is an expert in employment law compliance and HR documentation. He creates bespoke policies, contracts, and handbooks that balance legal requirements with commercial practicality, ensuring businesses have robust, compliant frameworks that work in practice.

Emily Roberts

Recruitment Support Specialist

Emily specializes in helping SMEs recruit effectively without the expense of agencies. She supports clients through job design, candidate assessment, interview structuring, and offer management, ensuring businesses hire the right people efficiently and compliantly.
