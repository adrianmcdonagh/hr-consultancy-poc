Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an outsourced HR service or ad hoc support?

It depends on your business needs. Outsourced HR provides ongoing strategic support and consistent advice, ideal for businesses with regular people management needs. Ad hoc support works well for specific projects or occasional guidance. We can discuss which approach suits you best during our discovery call.

Can you help with disciplinaries and grievances?

Yes. We provide comprehensive support for all employee relations matters, including disciplinaries, grievances, investigations, and capability issues. We guide you through fair process, help with documentation, attend meetings if needed, and ensure legal compliance throughout.

Do you create contracts and handbooks?

Absolutely. We create bespoke employment contracts, handbooks, and HR policies tailored to your business. These aren't generic templates – they reflect your culture, commercial needs, and legal requirements, giving you compliant documentation that actually works for your organisation.

How quickly can you start?

We can often start within a few days, depending on the nature of your requirement. For urgent matters such as ongoing employee relations cases or restructuring support, we prioritise getting support in place quickly.

Do you support redundancy processes?

Yes. We guide you through the full redundancy process, from initial planning and consultation through to settlement agreements and final documentation. Our support ensures legal compliance while handling these sensitive situations with care and professionalism.

What does retained HR support include?

Retained support typically includes unlimited advice via phone and email, template documents, policy updates, employment law updates, case management support, and strategic HR guidance. We tailor packages to your specific needs and team size.
