About Us

We're a people-first HR consultancy dedicated to supporting SMEs with pragmatic, compliant HR guidance they can trust.

Our Story

We founded our consultancy with a clear purpose: to provide SMEs with the same calibre of HR support that larger organisations enjoy, but delivered with the flexibility, accessibility, and pragmatism that growing businesses need.

Our approach is rooted in real-world experience working with owner-managed businesses and smaller teams. We understand the commercial pressures you face, the need to move quickly, and the importance of clear, actionable advice over theoretical frameworks.

Most of our work comes through referrals from clients who value our calm handling of sensitive situations, our commercial awareness, and our commitment to delivering outcomes that work in practice, not just on paper.

How We Work

Clear Advice

We explain complex employment law in plain English, giving you confidence to make informed decisions. No jargon, no ambiguity – just straightforward guidance you can act on.

Calm Handling of Tricky Issues

Employee relations matters can be stressful and emotional. We bring objectivity, experience, and a calm approach to help you navigate sensitive situations with professionalism and fairness.

Commercial Mindset

We understand business priorities. Our advice balances legal compliance with commercial reality, helping you protect your business while achieving your objectives efficiently.

Compliant Approach

Employment law compliance isn't optional. We ensure your policies, processes, and documentation meet legal requirements, reducing risk and protecting your business from potential claims.

Our Values

Integrity

We operate with honesty and transparency in every interaction. Our advice is objective, ethical, and always in your best interests.

Clarity

We communicate in plain language, making complex employment law accessible and actionable. You'll always understand your options and obligations.

Pragmatism

We focus on practical solutions that work in the real world of growing businesses, not just theoretical best practice that looks good on paper.

Partnership

We work alongside you as trusted partners, taking time to understand your business and building relationships based on trust and mutual respect.
